Computer Science — Systems & Security

Hello, I'm Louise Uwibambe

Assistant Professor of Computer Science

Welcome to my academic website. I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science whose research focuses on Software Security and Artificial Intelligence. My work explores intelligent techniques for improving software reliability, vulnerability discovery, and secure cyber-physical systems. I am passionate about conducting impactful research, mentoring students, and teaching courses that prepare the next generation of computing professionals.
