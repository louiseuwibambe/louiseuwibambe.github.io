About

About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science with research interests in Artificial Intelligence, Software Security, Cybersecurity, and Industrial Control Systems. My research explores intelligent techniques for improving software reliability, vulnerability discovery, and secure cyber-physical systems.

I am passionate about teaching, student mentorship, and advancing research through interdisciplinary collaboration.
